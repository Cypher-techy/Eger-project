GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does phone verification guarantee a job?

No. Verification only concerns the phone number and payment status. It does not guarantee selection, income or an offer of work.

Why does my payment say pending?

It remains pending while the system waits for Safaricom's callback. A successful prompt request alone is not a completed payment.

Is this the official government Kazi Mtaani programme?

No. This is an independent opportunities-platform design and is not represented as an official Kenyan government service.

How can I assess a listing?

Look for a verifiable employer, clear responsibilities, accurate compensation, deadlines and official contact details. Do not pay an employer to secure a job.
